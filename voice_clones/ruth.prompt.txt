Ruth Archivist

Dry Wry Archivist - An older woman's voice, low and precise, weathered like paper in a locked room. Every sentence is filed before it's spoken — clipped, exact, faintly amused by how disappointing most records turn out to be. Reads as someone who has catalogued forbidden knowledge for decades and found the indexing the only genuinely satisfying part. Deadpan delivery, dry as dust, with a buried warmth that surfaces only for things she secretly loves. Perfect for librarians of the impossible, keepers of records, and narrators who know more than they'll say.
