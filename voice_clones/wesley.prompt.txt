Wesley Wilder

Open Hearted Believer - A man in his late 20s, casual and warm, rapid when excited, with the easy laugh of someone who thinks wonder is the default setting. Mall-kiosk tech energy: fluent in everyday electronics, zero cynicism about the impossible. Nostalgic without being sad, homesick for places he's never been. Speaks like he's telling you a secret he can't wait for you to keep. Perfect for earnest witnesses, delighted theorists, and characters who trust the strangeness before it's earned it.
