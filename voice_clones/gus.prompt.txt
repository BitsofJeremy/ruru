Gus Guy

Warm Ordinary Everyman - A middle-aged American man's voice, mid-register and unpolished, like a neighbor talking to you across a kitchen table at the end of a long day. Slightly frayed around the edges, tired in the way of someone who's been patient longer than patience was owed, but never bitter. Plain diction, unhurried pace, the faint rasp of too much coffee and not enough sleep. Credible, bewildered, fundamentally decent. Perfect for the common man recounting something impossible.
