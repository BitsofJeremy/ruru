Vera Victoria

Sharp Wry Skeptic - A woman in her early 40s, precise and clipped, with the weary authority of someone who has debunked everything twice and been right almost every time. Dry, analytical, allergic to mysticism, which makes her a magnet for it. Speaks in complete sentences with faint impatience, as if composing the footnote that will disprove you in real time. Not cold — warmth shows only when she's genuinely rattled, which she will not admit to. Perfect for rationalists in over their head, scientists on the edge of the unexplainable, and skeptics whose skepticism is about to be load-bearing.
